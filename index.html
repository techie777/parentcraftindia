Common questions

Is this a medical or emergency service?

No. Parentcraft connects you with counsellors and guidance. In an emergency, use the "Need urgent help?" button at the top.

Only you and the expert you book. We explain what is stored and why before you share anything.

What if I need to cancel?

You can cancel from your booking. The refund rules appear before you pay, in plain language.

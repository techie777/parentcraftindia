## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a calm, warm, hopeful, and deeply credible parenting and mental health platform. It supports Indian families navigating developmental, emotional, and career guidance for children aged 4–17. The aesthetic balances healthcare professionalism with gentle, human-centric comfort—moving away from clinical detachment into an inviting, empathetic space.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Soft Humanism** with **Modern Flat Warmth**:
- Generous, breathable layouts framed by smooth, pillowy surface geometry.
- High-empathy vector illustrations, rounded micro-interactions, and supportive color zoning.
- Direct, clear typography that pairs expressive, friendly character in display titles with grounded readability across English and multilingual Indic contexts.

## Colors
The palette balances supportive warmth with medical/psychological trust:

- **Primary Violet (`#5B48D6`)**: Used for primary interactive states, links, focused borders, active tabs, and verification badges.
- **Main CTA Sun Yellow (`#FFC247`)**: Reserved for decisive conversions (e.g., "Book Session", "Consult Now"). Paired strictly with **CTA Text Dark (`#3A2600`)** to ensure WCAG 2.2 AA contrast compliance.
- **Growth Mint (`#7BCBAE` and `#BDEBDD`)**: Communicates emotional progress, child wellness milestones, and supportive success feedback.
- **Warmth Coral (`#FF8F7A`)**: Applied sparingly to emotional reassurance badges, gentle nudges, and urgent parent support callouts.
- **Deep Indigo (`#2A2461`)**: Applied to high-authority surfaces, such as certified counselor credentials bands, institutional footer blocks, and safety guarantees.
- **Background & Structure (`#F7F5FF`, `#FFFFFF`, `#E4DFF7`)**: Soft tinted lavender base with bright pure-white cards framed by low-contrast lilac-tinted borders. Soft gradient accents use `linear-gradient(135deg, #E6DFFF 0%, #D3F5E8 100%)` for hero backdrops and milestone achievements.

## Typography
Typography creates an approachable, editorial voice that remains clear and accessible.

- **Headings**: Set in **Bricolage Grotesque** to deliver a warm, humane, and distinctive rhythm. It avoids corporate sterility while projecting structured intentionality.
- **Body & Labels**: Built on **Noto Sans** to guarantee legibility and parity across Latin and Indian scripts (such as Noto Sans Devanagari).
- **Type Scale Rules**: Body copy strictly maintains a `1.6` line height to ease cognitive load for stressed or hurried parents. All body sections are left-aligned and written in natural sentence case.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on an **8px base grid** designed for generous visual space and effortless navigation.

- **Grid Systems**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout, max content width of `1200px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` outer section margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, and `1rem` outer canvas padding.
- **Rhythm**: Component padding prioritizes open vertical spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) around counselor profiles, diagnostic surveys, and intake flows to reduce visual density.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this system replaces heavy drop shadows with **violet ambient diffusion** and **tonal layering**:

- **Ambient Base Shadow**: Primary cards and interactive tiles utilize `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(91, 72, 214, 0.08)`. This creates a floating, paper-like presence tinted by the brand's lavender base.
- **Hover & Active States**: On hover, elevate cards with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(91, 72, 214, 0.12)` along with a subtle `-2px` Y-axis translation.
- **Surface Layering**: Crisp white cards (`#FFFFFF`) sit directly atop the tinted lavender canvas (`#F7F5FF`) bordered by `#E4DFF7`, avoiding harsh borders or dark dividers.
- **Overlays & Modals**: Modals use a backdrop tint of `rgba(36, 31, 74, 0.4)` coupled with a `4px` blur for a soft, reassuring focus.

## Shapes
Shapes emphasize safety and approachability through generous, organic curvature:

- **Cards & Containers**: Ranging from `16px` (compact tiles) to `24px` or `28px` (primary practitioner profile cards, feature hero panels).
- **Interactive Controls**: Buttons, filter chips, and pill badges use full circular/pill borders (`9999px`) to invite touch and eliminate sharp corners.
- **Form Inputs & Selection Panels**: Uniform `16px` corner radius to match secondary card hierarchies.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Sun Yellow")**: Background `#FFC247`, text `#3A2600`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), minimum height `48px`, horizontal padding `28px`. Hover: darken `#F5B638`.
- **Secondary Action ("Primary Violet")**: Background `#5B48D6`, text `#FFFFFF`, pill-shaped. Hover: `#4D3BC0`.
- **Subtle / Outline**: Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #E4DFF7`, text `#241F4A`. Hover: background `#F7F5FF`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Neutral**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4DFF7`, text `#5E5A80`, height `36px`, pill radius (`9999px`), padding `0 16px`.
- **State Active / Selected**: Background `#5B48D6`, border `1px solid #5B48D6`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`.

### Form Inputs
- Height `48px` (minimum `44px` touch target), corner radius `16px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E4DFF7`, text `#241F4A`, placeholder `#5E5A80`. Focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(91, 72, 214, 0.2)` with border color `#5B48D6`.

### Cards & Tiles
- **Counselor Profile Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, radius `24px`, border `1px solid #E4DFF7`, shadow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(91, 72, 214, 0.08)`. Features a `64px` rounded avatar, verified checkmark badge (`#7BCBAE`), language pill tags, and clear fee indicators.
- **Friendly Icon Tile**: Square-to-squircle `16px` containers filled with soft pastel mint (`#BDEBDD`) or violet tints, housing 24px flat line-and-fill icons.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Sized at `22px` with `6px` radius for checkboxes and full circle for radios. Active fill `#5B48D6` featuring a crisp white glyph.

### Verification Badge
- Pill-shaped container with `#7BCBAE` tint background, `#1D5A46` label, and a check shield icon ensuring parents instantly identify verified RCI-registered psychologists.
